Brand Kit & Guidelines

1 · Wordmark

The exact lockup from the physical packaging. Never redraw it, never restyle it per-post.

"Karmapath" line
Uppercase, regular weight — never bold, wide letter-spacing (0.16em), color #6D28D9 (violet).
"Sankalp" line
Uppercase, regular weight, even wider letter-spacing (0.5em), color #8A8A8A (mid gray), sits directly beneath "Karmapath," smaller size, indented slightly right.
Stacking order
KARMAPATH always on top, SANKALP always directly beneath — never side-by-side, never reversed.
Minimum size
Never smaller than 11px cap-height for "Karmapath" in any digital placement — below that, use the feather mark alone instead.
Backgrounds
Only on white, off-white (#F5F5F7), or very light neutral backgrounds. Never on a photo, never on a saturated color block.
Never
Never bold, never italic, never a drop shadow, never re-colored per emotion, never stretched or condensed.

2 · Feather Mark

Krishna's mor pankh, redrawn as a single continuous line. The signature, not the decoration.

Line weight
One consistent stroke weight throughout — no variable width, no taper.
Default color
Charcoal ink #2B2B2B on white/off-white. On emotion-specific assets, may shift to that emotion's accent color at 6–15% opacity only.
Placement size
Small, corner-anchored on all daily content. Larger and centered only on first-touch brand assets (page cover, greeting card, packaging, Day 1 manifesto slide).
Clear space
Minimum clear space on every side equal to the width of the feather's stem. Never crop the crown or stem.
Pairing with wordmark
When both appear together, feather sits above the wordmark as its "crest" — never beside it, never overlapping the letterforms.
Never
No gradients, no bevels/drop shadows, no second competing icon in frame, never scaled larger than the headline type on any single asset.

Sampled directly from the printed Karmapath packaging insert — a soft radial blush that reads as white from a distance but is never a flat, sterile white up close.

Shape
A soft radial gradient centered slightly above middle — dusty rose-lavender at the core, warming to near-white at the corners. Never a hard linear top-to-bottom fade.
CSS
radial-gradient(ellipse 90% 70% at 50% 42%, #EAD8DD 0%, #F3E8E6 45%, #FAF6F4 75%, #FFFFFF 100%)
Where to use it
Packaging, brand-reveal / manifesto assets, page and cover backgrounds, gifting cards. This IS the site's own background right now.
Where to use flat white instead
Straight product photography (catalog thumbnails, box flat-lays) stays on pure white or off-white #F5F5F7 for e-commerce trust and clarity — the blush gradient is a brand-storytelling background, not a product-shot background.

4 · Typography

Display / Headlines
Jost — uppercase, wide letter-spacing, regular weight for brand lines; medium (500–600) only for section headers, never for the wordmark itself.
Body / Captions
Work Sans — sentence case, no letter-spacing, 400–500 weight.
Sanskrit
Noto Serif Devanagari — always paired directly above or beside its English translation, never used alone without a translation nearby.
Never
No decorative or script fonts anywhere in the system. No all-caps body text (uppercase is reserved for headlines and the wordmark).

5 · Brand Vocabulary

Core phrases to rotate through captions, story text, and CTAs — never paraphrase these, use them verbatim.

Sankalp Assume the Role of Arjuna Let the Universe Guide You Observe Your Mind. Read When You Feel: Explore the Path Sent with light and intention.

Full-length line (use sparingly, as a closer):

"You deserve absolute clarity and peace. May this codex be a mirror to your inner wisdom."

7 · Content Production Rules

Reels
100% real, on-ground, filmed on an actual phone. 5–9 seconds. Silent or ASMR-only audio. No acting, no voiceover, no heavy editing. Hands and product only.
Feed Posts / Carousels
AI-generated static assets. Always off-white gradient background, one emotion accent, torn-paper texture, generous negative space, the brand lockup small in a corner.
Stories
AI-generated graphics layered with native Instagram interactive stickers (poll, slider, question box, quiz, countdown, mention). Every Story Day ends on a link sticker.
Consistency Check
Before publishing anything, confirm: correct emotion color, correct vibe/mood, brand lockup present and correctly weighted, and at least one vocabulary phrase in the caption.

8 · Master Do's & Don'ts

Always Do

  • Keep KARMAPATH regular weight, never bold
  • Use exactly one emotion color per asset
  • Pair every Sanskrit line with its English translation
  • Place the feather + wordmark lockup small, in a corner or bottom edge
  • Match lighting/mood to that emotion's fixed vibe
  • Film Reels 100% real and unscripted, 5–9 seconds
  • Close every Story Day with a link sticker

Never Do

  • Never bold, italicize, or re-color the wordmark
  • Never mix two emotion colors on one asset (except the 8-dot grid)
  • Never add a drop shadow, bevel, or gradient to the feather mark
  • Never scale the brand lockup larger than the headline type
  • Never act, use a voiceover, or heavily edit a Reel
  • Never place the wordmark on a photo or saturated color block
  • Never paraphrase the core brand phrases — use them verbatim
